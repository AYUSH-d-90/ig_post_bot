**Bio-Integrated Robotics Unveils Mycelial Networks as Scaffolding for Off-World Construction**

This cutting-edge research explores integrating living fungal mycelium into robotic frameworks, allowing them to autonomously grow and repair infrastructure in extreme environments like the Moon or Mars. Imagine lunar habitats or Martian outposts that can self-heal cracks and expand using biological processes, significantly reducing reliance on complex machinery and human intervention. This symbiotic approach promises a sustainable and adaptive future for extraterrestrial colonization by blurring the lines between advanced robotics and biology.
